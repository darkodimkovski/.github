# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive when collaborating in issues, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Focus feedback on the work, welcome questions, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information.

If you see a problem, use GitHub's content reporting tools or your organization's established private reporting channel. Do not post sensitive details in a public thread. Maintainers may moderate contributions that do not follow these expectations.
